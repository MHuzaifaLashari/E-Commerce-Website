    Vertical supply chain

    From yarn to your door, one supply chain.

    We manage each stage ourselves instead of buying finished goods through layers of middlemen. That’s why colours match from style to style, prices hold steady season after season, and the stock is already in our US warehouses when you order.

    1. 01

      Yarn & fabric

      Ring-spun cotton and cotton-poly blends knitted to our own weights and handle.

    2. 02

      Dye & finish

      Dyed to one colour standard, so a Jet Black hoodie matches a Jet Black tee.

    3. 03

      Cut & sew

      Made to our patterns and size specs for the same fit every season.

    4. 04

      Quality check

      Weight, size and shade checked batch by batch before anything ships.

    5. 05

      US warehouses

      Held in Kansas City, MO and Kingstree, SC — same-day shipping on orders before 3PM.

    • Colour you can matchOne colour standard across every style and brand.
    • Prices without the middleNo layers of margin between the factory and you.
    • Stock you can seeLive stock by warehouse for every colour and size once you log in.

    Sustainability

    Made to be worn, not wasted.

    The most sustainable garment is the one that gets worn for years. That idea shapes the fabrics we choose, how we build each piece and what we do with stock that sells slowly.

    • Built to last

      Midweight and heavyweight fabrics with details like double-fabric hoods and brushed fleece, made to be washed and worn again and again.

    • Nothing left behind

      Slow-moving colourways go to our online outlet at lower prices instead of sitting on shelves or being thrown away.

    • Fewer, fuller shipments

      Two US warehouses and one supply chain mean stock moves in planned, full loads rather than rushed part-shipments.

    • Responsible partners

      We work with long-term production partners and hold them to clear standards for people and the environment.
